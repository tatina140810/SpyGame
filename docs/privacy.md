# Privacy Policy — Spy Hunt: Word Party Game

**Effective Date:** May 21, 2026

We value your privacy. The Spy Hunt app does not collect, store or transmit
any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

## 1. Data we do not collect

The app does **not** collect any of the following:

- Names
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Location data
- Photos, contacts, or any personally identifiable information

## 2. Data we do not use

We do not use, analyse, or share any user data. All game activity takes
place locally on your device.

## 3. Advertising and analytics

The app does not use third-party advertising networks or analytics services.

## 4. Device permissions

The app may request access to **Local Network** access. This is used solely
to discover other phones in the same WiFi for the local multiplayer mode.
No data leaves your local network during multiplayer sessions.

The app does **not** request access to:

- Camera
- Microphone
- Contacts
- Location
- Health data
- Push notifications
- Any other sensitive features

## 5. Custom topics (premium feature)

If you use the "Add your topic" premium feature, the topic text you type is
sent to our own server (`spy-game-lac-seven.vercel.app`), which forwards it
to OpenAI to generate 40 game words. The topic and generated words are:

- not stored on our server (the request is processed and discarded);
- not linked to any user identifier (we don't have user accounts);
- not shared with any party other than OpenAI for the duration of the
  single request.

OpenAI's data handling is governed by their own policy:
<https://openai.com/policies/api-data-usage-policies>.

## 6. In-App Purchases

Premium features are unlocked via Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the
payment — we never see your payment details. The unlock state is stored
locally on your device.

## 7. Security

Since the app does not transmit or store personal data, there is no risk
of data leaks or breaches on our side.

## 8. Children

The app is rated 17+ because it contains adult-themed word categories. It
is not directed at children under 17.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: `tatinaiosdev@gmail.com`.
